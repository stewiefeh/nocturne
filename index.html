The philosophy

Nothing here is in a hurry

Iris root ages three years before it is distilled. Every batch macerates four weeks. The perfumer answers her own door. This is not a manifesto about slowness; it is simply how the perfume gets good.

See the whole process

One

Source close

Eleven of our materials come from three farms we have visited. The rest come from dealers we have argued with for a decade.

Two

Macerate fully

Four weeks minimum, tasted weekly, released only when the blotter agrees with the reference.

Three

Press small

Runs of 200 to 600 flacons. Numbered. When a batch is gone it is gone until the next pressing.
