The formula's story

Citrus perfumes usually die an hour in; Vesper Citrus was built to outlive dusk. Bitter orange and litsea give the opening its snap, basil a green undertone. Neroli is the heart, but immortelle — the curry-plant flower that smells of maple and hay — keeps the light going. An ambergris accord and vetiver in the base mean the sixth hour is as good as the first.
