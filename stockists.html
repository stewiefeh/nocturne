Where the cabinet travels

Six shops in five countries carry Nocturne, each chosen because someone there argued with the perfumer about fragrance and won at least once.

The Chemist & the Curator

Rembrandtplein 14, Amsterdam

Cabinet: all six. Sittings by the shop, quarterly.

Maison Horizon

Rue des Arquebusiers, Paris

Cabinet: First Rain, Ink Violet, Amber Vigil.

The Quiet Shelf

Marylebone Lane, London

Cabinet: all six. Hosts the annual winter tasting.

Salt & Cedar

Grand Marais, Minnesota

Cabinet: Cedar Silence, Vesper Citrus. Local pride, fairly earned.

Apotheca Moderna

Kyoto, Nakagyo ward

Cabinet: all six. Samples by appointment.

Folded Space

Lisbon, Principe Real

Cabinet: rotating. Currently Midnight Tuberose and Vesper Citrus.

Every shop, city, and arrangement above is invented. No such shops exist, which their proprietors would be relieved to learn.
